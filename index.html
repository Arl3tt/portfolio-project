Vanessa Diaz

I am a neuroscience graduate and AI engineer specializing in Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) and NeuroAI systems. My work focuses on building end-to-end platforms that translate neural signals into intelligent applications for communication, cognitive monitoring, and human‑AI interaction. I develop real-time neural data pipelines and machine learning models using Python, TensorFlow, and PyTorch, integrating EEG-based signal processing with modern cloud and edge computing technologies. My projects explore areas such as real-time BCI communication, neuroadaptive AI systems, cognitive state monitoring, and closed-loop neural feedback. Certified in AI engineering, TensorFlow development, and cloud-native technologies through IBM, Microsoft, and DeepLearning.AI, I focus on creating scalable, ethical neurotechnology that bridges neuroscience and artificial intelligence.
